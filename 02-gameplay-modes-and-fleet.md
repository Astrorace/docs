# Gameplay Modes, Ship Classes & Modular Engineering

## 1. Nine Complete Playable Modes & Competitive Systems

1. **Quick Race (`QUICK`):** 2–4 pilots with full custom builds and `< 4.5s` hybrid matchmaking. If live human slots do not fill within 4.5 seconds, human-calibrated **Sim-Pilot AI drivers** populate remaining grid slots using identical 60 Hz physics and capacitor limits (**zero artificial rubberbanding**).
2. **Ranked Trophy Ladder (`RANKED`):** Six competitive leagues (*Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Grandmaster*) paired with 4-player **Glicko-2 MMR** and **MMR Daily Fatigue** (`100%` MMR weight for races 1–30/day, `50%` for 31–50, `15%` for 51+), preventing energy refills from buying leaderboard dominance.
3. **30-Minute Swiss Flash Cups (`FLASH`):** Automated 16-pilot, 4-round tournaments firing every 30 minutes where all ships normalize to `T1 Common L1`—100% pure racing line and combat timing.
4. **Co-op Leviathan Boss Raid (`BOSS`):** 4 pilots intercept the 400-meter rogue mining dreadnought **Leviathan MK-IV** (`9,000 HP`, 3-phase laser sweep, gravity vortex, and exposed reactor core) in a 60-second orbital raid.
5. **Interactive Flight School (`PILOTING` & `COMBAT`):** Zero-energy server-validated onboarding lessons rewarding an Epic starter thruster upon graduation.
6. **Daily Prototype X-7 Test Drive (`TEST_DRIVE`):** Daily solo trial of an upgraded `Epic +5` build with pre-race delta telemetry.
7. **8-Sector Syndicate Territory Wars:** 30-pilot clans (`[TAG]`) share daily Warp Cell energy (`+5 cells/day`) and compete for weekly control over 8 orbital sectors (`+10% CC` bonus and Seasonal Championship Vault eligibility).
8. **Private Creator Lobbies & Live Spectator:** Instant room codes allowing Twitch, YouTube, Kick, and X creators to race live with viewers in one click.
9. **Deterministic Match Replays:** Server-archived tick playback (`1x` / `2x` speed, timeline scrubbing, verified settlement receipts).

## 3. Six-Slot Engineering & Strict Anti-Pay-to-Win Gates

Every ship is assembled across **6 physical equipment slots** (*Hull, Thruster, Shield, Handling, Capacitor, Weapon*) spanning **5 Tiers (`T1–T5`)**, **5 Rarities (`Common` to `Legendary`)**, and **10 Enhancement Levels (`L1–L10`)**.

- **Physical Trade-Offs:** Heavy weapons and reinforced plating add mass and capacitor draw, reducing acceleration and cornering agility.
- **League Equipment Gates:** Higher-tier hardware cannot be equipped until the pilot earns the corresponding Ranked League (`Bronze = T1`, `Silver = T2`, `Gold = T3`, `Platinum = T4`, `Diamond/Grandmaster = T5`).
- **Transparent PRD Pity System:** High-tier module containers enforce Pseudo-Random Distribution with Soft Pity starting at crate 41 (`+4.875%` per step) and guaranteed Hard Pity at crate 60 (`100% Legendary`).
