## 2. Core Economic Principles & Deflationary Mechanics

### Zero Gameplay Minting
`$ATRC` cannot be farmed, minted, or printed from daily logins or regular match completions. Core Free-to-Play progression operates on off-chain `Cosmic Credits (CC)` and salvageable `Nanites`. Skilled F2P pilots earn `$ATRC` organically by winning scarce tradable modules in competitive play and selling them peer-to-peer to collectors and competitors on the open marketplace.

### P2P Module Marketplace & 5.0% Clearing Protocol (`M11`)
`$ATRC` serves as the exclusive settlement currency for trading scarce `Rare`, `Epic`, `Legendary`, and capped **`Numbered Series (№001–300)`** ship modules (unlocked at Pilot Level 15). Every P2P transaction executes an automated **5.0% clearing split**:
- **95.0%** — Instant settlement to the Seller
- **2.5%** — **Permanently Burned** (mathematically contracting total `$ATRC` supply on every trade)
- **2.5%** — **Seasonal Syndicate Championship Treasury** (funding recurring esports prize pools from real market velocity rather than token inflation)

Because every wash trade forfeits 5.0% of principal ($C_N = C_0 \times 0.95^N$), 14 round-trip wash trades destroy **51.2%** of a bad actor's capital directly into permanent supply burn and honest competitors' tournament vaults.

### Additional `$ATRC` Ecosystem Utility Sinks
- **Apex Quantum Containers (`250 ATRC`):** High-tier module drops backed by PRD Soft/Hard Pity (`41 / 60`).
- **Seasonal Premium Pass (`500 ATRC`) & High-Stakes Esports Flash Cups (`30 ATRC` buy-in).**
- **Streamer & Creator Flywheel:** Automated **10% `$ATRC` revenue share** on referred community ecosystem activity.
